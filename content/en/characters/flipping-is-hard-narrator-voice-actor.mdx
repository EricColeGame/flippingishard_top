### Why Reaction Beats Instruction

Many games use a narrator to teach mechanics. Flipping is Hard flips that expectation: the voice reacts to what you *did*, not what you *should* do. The result is a narrator who feels like a companion watching your run, which is a much harder tone to sustain than straightforward tutorial guidance. It also means repeated failures stay entertaining, since each disaster gets its own reaction.

### What This Means If You Want to Voice a Character

If you are an aspiring voice actor, this style of casting is genuinely accessible. You do not need a professional booth to submit a strong read — you need a clean recording, a clear character choice, and a performance that stands out in under a minute.

- **Lead with character, not volume.** A distinct take beats a loud one.
- **Record clean audio.** Treat your space before you buy gear.
- **Keep it short and confident.** Roughly 100 words is a tight window; make the first line count.
- **Match the game's tone.** Theatrical, warm, and playful is the target register.
- **Follow the game's channels.** Casting news tends to surface there first.
