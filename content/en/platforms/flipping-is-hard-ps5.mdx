## Tips for Your First Climb (Whichever Platform You Land On)

While the Flipping is Hard PS5 question stays open, here's how to make the PC version less punishing.

- **Turn on Chill Mode first.** It enables checkpoints and turns a rage game into a manageable one. You can always switch it off once you've learned the movement.
- **Respect the vibration jump.** It's your only way to gain height, and the timing window is tight. Practice it in a safe corner of the first area before climbing.
- **Use quick restarts.** The game is built for fast retries with built-in splits, so a bad attempt costs seconds, not minutes.
- **Play co-op with purpose.** Beams and microwaves exist to help friends — coordinate instead of free-climbing past each other.
- **Chase the 16 NPC challenges.** These side quests are movement tests with rewards, and they'll sharpen the skills the main climb demands.
- **Check the language support.** Interface and subtitles are available in English, French, German, Japanese, and Korean; full audio is English only.

Community reports suggest the difficulty curve spikes hard around the mid-game areas, so don't be discouraged if progress stalls. Falling is the mechanic, not a failure state.
