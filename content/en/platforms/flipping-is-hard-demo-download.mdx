## Co-op, Chill Mode, and Replay Value

Where the game separates itself from a standard rage platformer is the multiplayer layer. Online co-op supports up to 16 players, and the tools are designed around helping each other rather than racing — beams and microwaves can be used to give friends a leg up. For solo players, the optional Chill Mode enables checkpoints and softens the climb considerably. It's the right choice if you want the story and the jokes without the repeated falls. Speedrunners get the opposite treatment: quick restarts and built-in splits for timing runs.
